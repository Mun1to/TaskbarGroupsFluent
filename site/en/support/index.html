Support

Something not working as it should? Here is how to get help and report a bug, the questions that always come up, where your groups live, how to remove the app completely and, if it is useful to you, how to buy me a coffee.

Get help or report a bug

Everything goes through the GitHub issues, which are public: that way anyone with the same problem finds the answer. Check first whether it is already there; if not, open a new one. They get answered, in Spanish or in English.

Report a bug See the ones already reported

So it can be fixed the first time, include:

  • The app version. It is at the bottom right of the main window, in the quick bar.
  • Your Windows: 10 or 11, and its version (Win + R, type winver and press Enter).
  • Your taskbar: the Windows one, or Start11, StartAllBack or ExplorerPatcher, which draw their own.
  • How you installed it: from this website, with WinGet or from the Microsoft Store.
  • What you did, what you expected and what happened, step by step, so it can be repeated.
  • Your screens, if the panel shows up in the wrong place: how many you have and the scale of each one (Settings → System → Display).

The questions that always come up

The group does not open on hover

It ships switched off. Turn it on in the quick bar at the bottom of the main window, or in the menu of its icon by the clock. With Start11 or StartAllBack you need version 1.10 or later, which recognises the group by its icon.

Windows says «unknown publisher»

The installer is not signed with a paid certificate yet: click More info and then Run anyway. If you would rather not, the Microsoft Store version is signed by Microsoft.

How do I update it?

It updates itself: when it opens it asks GitHub whether there is a new version and offers it to you. With WinGet, winget upgrade Mun1to.TaskbarGroupsFluent; the Microsoft Store one is updated by the Store. What each version brings, in what's new.

Not here? The rest of the questions are on the home page.

Where your groups live

Nothing leaves your computer (the privacy page explains it). This is what it writes, and where:

  • Your groups, their icons and your settings, in %APPDATA%\Jack Schierbeck\taskbar-groups. It is the folder of the original Taskbar Groups, kept on purpose so your groups carry over between it and its forks.
  • The app itself, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\TaskbarGroupsFluent: it installs for your user, not the system.

To open one of those folders, paste its name, % signs included, into the File Explorer address bar. In the Microsoft Store version, Windows keeps your groups inside the app's package, and deletes them when you uninstall it.

How to uninstall it

First, unpin your groups from the taskbar (right-click → Unpin from taskbar): without the app they open nothing.

  • From this website or with WinGet: Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Taskbar Groups Fluent → Uninstall (on Windows 10, Apps and features).
  • From the Microsoft Store: right-click Taskbar Groups Fluent in the Start menu → Uninstall. Windows removes your groups with it.

With WinGet, from the terminal too:

winget uninstall Mun1to.TaskbarGroupsFluent

The uninstaller does not touch your groups: they stay in their folder, ready if you install it again. To remove them too, delete %APPDATA%\Jack Schierbeck\taskbar-groups.
