## English

**Product name:** Taskbar Groups Fluent

**Short description**

Group your apps into a single taskbar icon. Click it, or just rest the cursor on it, and a Windows 11 glass panel shows every app in the group.

**Description**

Ten icons on your taskbar? Now just one.

Taskbar Groups Fluent packs a set of apps into one taskbar button. Click it, or just rest the cursor on it, and a panel with real Windows 11 glass opens with every app in the group, so the taskbar stays tidy and everything you use is one click away.

Make each group your own:
• Add separators: on the taskbar to keep your groups apart from other pinned icons, and inside a panel to split it into sections.
• Lay its panel out as a grid, a horizontal row, a vertical column or a list, with or without app names, in four icon sizes.
• Show the group's name above or below, with a pencil that opens its editor right there.
• Use an image, a colour emoji or an icon made of the group's own apps.
• Mix apps, folders and even other groups, which open beside the panel like a submenu, and open them all with one button.
• Or have the panel show a folder, read again each time it opens: any folder, or a Windows place like the Control Panel, This PC, the Recycle Bin or all your apps.
• Set out a whole taskbar from one group: a group that holds groups pins them all, one after another, in its order and with its separators.
• Keep a group by the clock too, with its own icon in the notification area, one click away from its panel.
• Rename an app inside a group, give it an icon of its own or launch arguments, or run it as administrator.

Adding apps is easy: one searchable list of everything installed, desktop programs, Microsoft Store apps and your browser's web apps alike, with the right icons, or just drag files and shortcuts in from File Explorer. Groups can be exported to a single file, imported on another PC, or duplicated. Coming from Taskbar Groups or AppGroup? Import your groups and carry on.

It follows your Windows theme, accent colour and language (English, Spanish, Russian or Simplified Chinese), or the ones you pick in its quick bar, installs for your user only without administrator rights, and has no account, no telemetry and no ads. It is free and open source (MIT).

**What's new in this version**

Each app in a group can wear an icon of its own, and a browser's web app, the kind you install from Edge or Chrome, now shows its own icon and name and is listed in the app picker. Clicking a pinned group while its panel is open now closes it, like the Start menu. Saving a group no longer reloads the taskbar unless its icon or its name really changed, and even then it asks first. With Start11 or StartAllBack, open on hover now finds the group by its icon, even on a taskbar two rows high, and a right click on a group's icon no longer sets it off. A group that shows the Recycle Bin can empty it or restore everything in it, and a group's icon in the main window can be dragged onto the taskbar to pin it. A group dragged onto the taskbar opens on hover straight away with StartAllBack or Start11, and a click waits for the panel kept loaded instead of loading another when the PC is busy. The app now speaks Simplified Chinese too, translated by WindAndWood, and the program by the clock keeps a log of what it sees, to fix open on hover on a PC where it does not respond. A click now opens the panel over its own taskbar button and sized for that screen, the group editor has a File button for files and shortcuts, and the installer speaks Simplified Chinese, all three from WindAndWood. Renaming a group by changing only its capital letters no longer deletes it.

## 简体中文

Since 1.10.8 the package declares zh-Hans (PR #29), so Partner Center asks for this listing. Its screenshots are the English ones, in `screenshots/en`.

**产品名称：** Taskbar Groups Fluent

**简短说明**

把多个应用收进任务栏上的一个图标。点击它，或者只是把鼠标停在上面，就会弹出带有 Windows 11 毛玻璃效果的面板，显示组里的所有应用。

**说明**

任务栏上有十个图标？现在只需要一个。

Taskbar Groups Fluent 把一组应用收进任务栏上的一个按钮。点击它，或者只是把鼠标停在上面，就会打开一个带有真正 Windows 11 毛玻璃效果的面板，列出组里的所有应用。任务栏保持整洁，常用的东西一键即达。

按你的方式定制每个组：
• 添加分隔符：在任务栏上把你的组和其他固定的图标分开，在面板里把它分成几个部分。
• 面板可以排成网格、横排、竖排或列表，显示或不显示应用名称，图标有四种大小。
• 在上方或下方显示组名，旁边的铅笔可以直接打开它的编辑器。
• 用一张图片、一个彩色表情符号，或者由组里的应用拼成的图标作为组图标。
• 混合放入应用、文件夹，甚至其他组（它们像子菜单一样在面板旁边打开），还能一键全部打开。
• 或者让面板显示一个文件夹，每次打开时重新读取：任意文件夹，或者控制面板、此电脑、回收站、所有应用等 Windows 位置。
• 用一个组布置整条任务栏：包含其他组的组会按顺序、连同分隔符，把它们依次固定到任务栏上。
• 也可以把一个组放在时钟旁边，在通知区域有它自己的图标，一键打开它的面板。
• 重命名组里的应用，给它单独的图标或启动参数，或者以管理员身份运行。

添加应用很简单：一个可搜索的列表包含所有已安装的程序，桌面程序、Microsoft Store 应用和浏览器的网页应用都在里面，图标也都正确；也可以直接从文件资源管理器拖入文件和快捷方式。组可以导出为单个文件、在另一台电脑上导入，或者复制。用过 Taskbar Groups 或 AppGroup？导入你的组，接着用。

它会跟随 Windows 的主题、强调色和语言（英语、西班牙语、俄语或简体中文），也可以在快捷栏里自己选择；只为当前用户安装，不需要管理员权限，没有账号、没有遥测、没有广告。完全免费并且开源（MIT）。

**此版本的新功能**

点击已固定的组时，面板现在会出现在它自己的任务栏按钮上方，大小也适合那块屏幕；组编辑器多了一个“文件”按钮，用来添加文件和快捷方式；安装程序也有了简体中文。这三项都来自 WindAndWood。只改大小写来重命名组，不会再删掉它。现在支持简体中文（由 WindAndWood 翻译）：应用、面板和菜单会跟随 Windows 的语言，也可以在快捷栏里选择“简中”。时钟旁边的程序现在会记录它看到的情况，方便在“悬停打开”没有反应的电脑上找出原因。之前的 1.10 版本还带来了：每个应用可以有自己的图标，从 Edge 或 Chrome 安装的网页应用会显示自己的图标和名称；面板打开时再点一次已固定的组就会关闭它；组可以显示回收站，并能清空或全部还原；可以把组的图标从主窗口拖到任务栏上来固定；使用 Start11 或 StartAllBack 时，悬停打开会通过图标认出组。
